About Bandhan Seva International

Founders Of BSI - Adhil & Rita

Adhil & Rita — Your Bridge Between Bangladesh and India

Rita is Bangladeshi. Adhil is Indian. We got married in August 2024.

One evening, Rita told me about the struggles Bangladeshi patients face when they travel to India for treatment. The language barrier after Kolkata. The confusion at railway stations. Not knowing who to trust. The fear of being overcharged. The loneliness of being in a strange country while sick.

"If only there was proper assistance from the moment they leave Dhaka to the moment they return, it would change everything for these families."

That conversation became Bandhan Seva International.

Adhil — India

Medical education background. Based in India. Handles hospital coordination, doctor appointments, and logistics on the ground.

Rita — Bangladesh

MBA graduate. Based in Bangladesh. Handles patient communication in Bengali, visa guidance, and family support from Dhaka.

  • We are a family, not a company. Indian husband + Bangladeshi wife.
  • We understand both sides — the patient's fear and the hospital's system.
  • Hospital selection and doctor appointments are free for patients.
  • We are based in Dhaka — you can meet us face-to-face before you travel.
  • We have direct partnerships with KIMS Health, Trivandrum and VPS Lakeshore, Kochi.

We treat every patient like family. Because we know what it feels like when someone you love is sick, and you don't know where to turn.
